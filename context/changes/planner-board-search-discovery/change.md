## Notes

<!-- Free-form notes for this change: links, ad-hoc context, decisions that don't belong in research/frame/plan. -->


Our planner board could be fully packed with information. We could have a dozen bundles that host subjects. This approach could be a challenge for the user to find some particular intriguing insight. For instance, users may want to know where all the math subjects are located, how the plan appears from the perspective of a specific teacher or student, or how it looks when considering a combination of both. Let's evaluate the feasibility of adding some kind of highlighting discovery feature.
